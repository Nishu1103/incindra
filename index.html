Approach

The problem was never the data. It's the connections.

Emergency notes, therapy charts, imaging and bills sit with different providers in different systems. Each fact is a data point. The case turns on the causal links between them, and no one system records those.

See how it works →

Every field with siloed records has this problem. Incindra starts where the stakes are highest: medical records in injury litigation.

It all starts with the chronology

Everything you expect from a chronology, and beyond.

Every matter begins with a complete, cited chronology of the record. It covers what counsel needs from any chronology, then goes further: how the findings connect and what the record is missing.

Connected, not listed

Each finding shows its causal links to what led to it and what followed, so you see how the case unfolded, not just when things happened.
