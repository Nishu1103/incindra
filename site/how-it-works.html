How it works

From scattered records to one connected case

Incindra puts every claim in the record under fire. It flags what misses the mark, burns away unsupported reasoning and misstatements, exposes the gaps, and maps the causal links that hold.

The same six steps run on every matter, and every run is versioned, so the same records on the same versions reproduce the same analysis.

  1. Gather

    Records from every provider, plus the patient's past records.

  2. Extract

    Every fact becomes a data point, cited to its page.

  3. Match

    Facts are lined up across providers and against the patient's past records.

  4. Build the knowledge graph

    Data points connected by causal links.

  5. Filter

    Each causal link is tested against the clinical taxonomy. The cited record stays the source of truth.

  6. Output

    Chronology, executive summary and analyses, cited to the page.

It all starts with the chronology

Everything you expect from a chronology, and beyond.

Every matter begins with a complete, cited chronology of the record. It covers what counsel needs from any chronology, then goes further: how the findings connect and what the record is missing.

  • Case overview and patient profile
  • Provider, diagnosis, treatment and medications for every entry
  • Test and imaging results
  • Every entry linked to its source page, handwritten notes included
  • Billing summary
  • Gaps in care and missing records, with a list of what to request
  • Pre-existing conditions separated from new injuries
  • Work status and future care
  • Search and filter by date, provider or symptom; export to Word or PDF
