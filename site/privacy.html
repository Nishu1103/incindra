Privacy notice

How Incindra handles the information you send through the demo request form.

What the form collects

When you request a demo, the form collects your name, your email address, your firm or practice, the role you select and anything you write about the matter. It also records which page of the site you sent it from.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your request.

Who processes it

The form is run by Formspree, a third-party form service. Formspree receives and processes each submission and passes it on to us.

Please don't send patient information

Don't include patient names, medical details or any other health information in the form. If you included any by mistake, email us at CONTACT_EMAIL.

Fonts

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when you open a page.

Contact

Questions about this notice? Email CONTACT_EMAIL.
