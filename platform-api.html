Incindra Platform API · for ADR, claims and review platforms

Your platform drafts. Incindra checks the evidence.

AI-led arbitration, claims and case-review platforms now draft reasoning and awards in minutes. Incindra's API adds the two things those drafts need before a human signs them: medical evidence reasoned from the record, and fixed checks that catch what language models miss.

  1. Parties submitRecords, statements and exhibits
  2. Your platform draftsIssues, analysis and a proposed outcome
  3. Incindra APIMedical evidence, release checks, consistency
  4. Human reviewsCounsel, arbitrator, mediator or medical expert decides
  5. IssuedWith an AI-use statement for the record
  • POST/v1/records/analyze

    Medical chronology and causal links

    Send the medical records; receive a cited chronology, the causal links between findings, conflicts between providers and missing records, as structured data your platform can display or reason over.

  • POST/v1/drafts/validate

    Release checks

    Send any draft with its exhibits. Totals are recomputed, every citation is confirmed to exist and say what it is cited for, dates are matched to their source, and each figure is checked against the party it belongs to. Failures come back with their exact location.

  • POST/v1/consistency

    Consistency testing

    Run the same case through your system repeatedly and get an agreement rate for each finding, measured against how often two human reviewers agree on the same record.

  • GET/v1/jobs/{id}/disclosure

    AI-use statement

    A plain-language record of what the system did, what the human did and the versions used, ready to attach to an award, report or file.
